TrustAge

Fraudsters can steal a password, but not time.

Our detector checks every online transfer before the money leaves. It asks one simple question: what on the path to the money is new?

Step 1

Measure time

How old is the device, the phone number, the payee? New things add points.

Step 2

Buy time

Not sure? Hold the payment for 1 hour. The customer can cancel it.

Step 3

Use time

During that hour, other payments, the owner and an analyst can expose the fraud.

Account takeover (ATO): a criminal gets into a customer's online bank account, or tricks the customer, and sends the money away.

Assumptions and business reasoning

What we assume, and why it is worth it

What we assume

  • The payment hub can ask the detector before sending money. The answer comes in under 0.3 seconds.
  • LDB can load 2 years of older transfers, so not every payee looks new on day one.
  • 2 fraud analysts in office hours, plus 1 person on call at night and weekends.
  • LDB's terms say a payment may be stopped for a safety check (EU payment law, PSD2 Art. 79).
  • Every point value is a first guess, tested silently for 6 weeks before it affects anyone.

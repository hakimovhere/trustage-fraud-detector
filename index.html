Luxembourg Digital Bank · Account-takeover fraud detector

TrustAge

Fraudsters can steal a password, but not time.

TrustAge checks every online transfer before the money leaves. It asks one simple question: what on the path to the money is new? A new device, a new phone number or a new payee adds points. When the detector is not sure, it buys time.

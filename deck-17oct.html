Detection logic · how we assign points

One formula sets every point

points = 7 × log₂(F ÷ G)

F = % of frauds with the signal
G = % of genuine payments with it

+7 points = odds × 2 · 70 points ≈ 1,000×

Example · new payee

70% ÷ 8% = 8.75× likelier
7 × log₂(8.75) = 21.9 → +20 points
